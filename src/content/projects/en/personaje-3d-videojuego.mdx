## Goal

My bachelor's thesis in Multimedia Engineering: design and build an original 3D character for an action game prototype, going through the whole production pipeline, from the idea to the engine. I started with no training in drawing or concept art.

## Process

- **Design:** silhouettes in Krita and reference boards in PureRef (Japanese clothing, hairstyles, weapons, combat poses).
- **Modelling in Blender:** a female base body built with polygon modelling; a sculpted mask with a side cut that reveals part of the face; hair made with hair cards; a katana in low and high poly versions; kimono, trousers, bandages, a belt with braided cords and waraji sandals.
- **PBR texturing** in Substance 3D Painter: 4K baking and cloth, skin and metal materials with wear, dirt and blood.
- **Rig** with Rigify and manual weight correction with Weight Painting.
- **Animation:** five Mixamo clips (idle, hurt walk, crouched sneak and two katana attacks) retargeted to the rig with Rokoko Studio Live.
- **Unity (URP):** Animator Controller, a control script for keyboard or gamepad and a third-person camera with Cinemachine.

## Technical decisions

- **Low-to-medium density mesh** from the start, without relying on subdivision, to keep control of the topology and the polygon count.
- **High → low poly katana:** the detail of bevels and reliefs is baked into textures to keep the geometry light.
- **No facial bones in the rig**, since the character has no facial animation. Rigid pieces (mask, hair, cords, katana) are parented to a bone and do not deform.
- **Reusable retargeting:** the bone mapping between Mixamo and Rigify was set up once, exported to JSON and applied to the remaining animations.
- **Movement by script** instead of Root Motion, which did not read some of the animations correctly. Movement is relative to the camera, with smoothed transitions between states.

## Result

A Windows build of the prototype in which the character explores a level, switches between walking and sneaking, and performs two attacks, with an orbiting third-person camera.
